# Candidate email templates

Short, warm, and respectfully direct. Fill the brackets; cut anything that
doesn't apply. Write every time in office time (PT) and label it. The calendar
invite carries the time zone, so the candidate's calendar shows their own time.

## 1. Onsite itinerary (send once the loop is locked)

The scheduler drafts this one automatically (`*-itinerary.md`). Checklist
before sending:

- Every round has a backup interviewer (see "Backup plan" in `schedule.md`).
- Travel: a guest booking link (like Navan), refundable fares.
- Preferred hotels near the office.
- What to bring (laptop? slides?) and who will greet them in the lobby.
- Anything about the office that would surprise a first-time visitor goes in
  `office_notes` (for example, a shoes-off office: ask their shoe size and have
  a fresh pair waiting).
- A reminder goes out the day before.

## 2. Same-day reschedule (an interviewer dropped out)

Order of operations: check the round's backup plan in `schedule.md` first; if
the backup can cover, the candidate may not need to hear about it at all. If
the day changes, tell them before they notice a gap.

> Subject: Small change to today's schedule
>
> Hi [First name],
>
> Quick heads-up: [Interviewer] had something urgent come up, so we've
> [moved your [round] to [new time] / brought in [backup], who works on
> [area]]. Everything else stays the same. [In the gap, [teammate] would love
> to grab coffee with you.]
>
> Sorry for the shuffle, and thanks for rolling with it.
>
> [Your name]

If the round can't happen today: tell the interviewer and the candidate, offer a short video call
later in the week (the scheduler's "Next move" line suggests a time), then
update the calendar and the ATS.

## 3. Warm rejection after an onsite

Finalists get a personal email or a short call from the recruiter or hiring
manager, never only a template. Send within a few business days of the debrief.
In a small research community, a respectful "no" protects the team's
reputation.

> Subject: Your [Role] interviews at [Company]
>
> Hi [First name],
>
> Thank you for the time and thought you put into your onsite. The team really
> enjoyed [one specific thing, e.g. your walkthrough of X].
>
> After a lot of discussion, we've decided not to move forward for this role.
> It was a close call, and [optional: we were impressed by your work on X].
>
> We'd love to stay in touch as we grow. [If true: I've noted you for future
> roles like [role], and here's where we post new openings: [link].]
>
> Wishing you the best,
> [Name]

Then tag them in the ATS for future roles if they were strong-but-not-now.
